What makes RetroGenY different

Play with friends

Connect two to four phones and battle, trade or race, the way these games were meant to be played.

  • GB/GBC link for two, GBA multiplayer for up to four
  • Same Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot, Bluetooth or a USB-C cable
  • Each phone gets its own colored cable; tap one to connect
  • MirrorLink keeps things smooth on a slow connection

Extended codes: cheats that go further

Our own cheat format goes where GameShark and Action Replay can't: data behind pointers, encrypted money, whole bags and boxes.

  • Follows pointers, so a code works in every save
  • Loops, conditions, button triggers and arithmetic
  • Checked as you type, explained in plain language, with templates
  • The whole built-in library of 9,000+ cheats uses it

Memory tools: make your own cheats

Find any number in a floating window over the game, then turn it into a cheat that survives a new save.

  • Search by value, or by changed / increased / decreased
  • Watchpoints write pointer-based cheats for you
  • Change what the game does, straight from the instructions that wrote a value
  • Luck control: force every random roll to the best result
