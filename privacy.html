Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

RetroGenY ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

The app itself collects no personal information

  • Your games, saves, save states, screenshots and covers stay on your phone, in the folder you chose or in the app's private storage. The only exception is cloud saves: if you turn them on, your game saves are copied to your own Google Drive (see below). Nothing is ever uploaded to us.
  • The app has no accounts of its own and never asks for your name, email or phone number. (The optional RetroAchievements login uses your RetroAchievements account; see below.)
  • The app has no servers of its own. To find and fix crashes, and to see how the app is used, it sends crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase (see below).

Link play

When you link, your phone talks directly to the other phones over the same local network, Bluetooth or a cable, never through our servers. What's exchanged is the data the games need to link, plus your device name so the other players can recognize you. For Bluetooth play the system asks for the "Nearby devices" permission, used only to find and connect to the other phone.

Logs made with "Export link log" stay on your phone. We only see one if you choose to send it to us.

Cloud saves with Google Drive (optional)

Cloud saves are off until you connect Google Drive in Settings. When they are on:

  • The app copies your game saves (.sav files) to a folder called "RetroGenY Saves" in your own Google Drive, and copies newer saves back from it, so you can continue on another phone or after reinstalling. Save states, games, screenshots and covers are not uploaded.
  • The app asks Google only for the drive.file permission. With it the app can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Google Drive.
  • The files travel directly between your phone and Google over an encrypted connection. We never receive your saves, and the app does not read your Google account's name or email address.
  • To keep the sync records, the app stores on your phone a checksum of each save and the ID of its copy on Google Drive.

Disconnect in Settings and the app stops syncing. Your saves stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there. To withdraw the app's access completely, remove RetroGenY under Third-party apps & services in your Google Account. Google handles your Drive data under the Google Privacy Policy.

RetroGenY's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Exporting and importing saves

"Export save" hands the save file to the app you pick (email, a chat app, a cloud drive) through Android's share sheet; where it goes from there is up to you. "Import save" only reads the file you pick.

RetroAchievements (optional)

The app only talks to RetroAchievements (retroachievements.org, an independent third-party service) if you turn RetroAchievements on in Settings and log in. What it sends:

  • Your RetroAchievements username and login token. Your password is sent to RetroAchievements once, when you log in, to get the token; the app never stores it.
  • An identifier for the current game: a checksum (MD5) calculated from the game file. The file itself is never uploaded.
  • The achievements you unlock, leaderboard scores, and a one-line description of what you're doing in the game (for example "On stage 3"), which appear on your RetroAchievements profile.
  • The app name and version, and your Android version.

The login token is stored on your phone. Log out or turn RetroAchievements off in Settings and nothing more is sent. RetroAchievements handles this data under its own terms; see RetroAchievements' terms and privacy information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your choice through Google's consent tool before showing ads, and you can choose non-personalized ads.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase succeeded, to unlock features.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase, a Google service for app developers:

  • Crashlytics: when the app crashes, it sends a crash report: where in the app it happened, the app and Android version, the phone model, and a random installation ID. It contains no games, saves or personal information.
  • Analytics: anonymous usage statistics, such as which console a game is for (GBA, GB or GBC), which screen renderer is used, and whether link play, ads or the purchase were used. Game names are not sent. Firebase collects a random app-instance ID and device information (model, Android version, language, approximate country from the IP address). Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and the UK), statistics follow the choice you make in the consent form.
  • Remote Config: the app downloads a few settings from Firebase (for example how often ads appear), so they can change without an app update.

The usage statistics are also shared with Google AdMob, which shows us how much ad revenue the app earns; they are not used to personalize ads. We use this data only to fix problems and improve the app; it is not sold, and we do not use it to identify you. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
